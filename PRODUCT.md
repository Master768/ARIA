# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Analyst managers, product managers, and data analysts who need to review, edit, and approve multi-agent analytics reports. Their primary job is to check verification results and sign off on reports for decision-making.

## Product Purpose
Orchestra is a multi-agent analytics assistant. The web dashboard acts as a human-in-the-loop gate where draft reports are reviewed, Critic validation outcomes are inspected, markdown edits are made, and final approved reports are archived (as Markdown and DOCX).

## Brand Personality
Professional & Trustworthy. The interface should feel structured and reliable, utilizing clear visual hierarchy, readable data layouts, and prominent color-coded badges (green for success, red for errors) to convey correctness.

## Anti-references
Generic stock Streamlit layouts, low-contrast text on colored badges, unformatted numeric listings, cluttered sidebars, and chaotic structural grids.

## Design Principles
1. **Evidence First:** Numeric correctness and validation logs (from the Critic Agent) must be immediately visible and clear.
2. **Typographic Rhythm:** Report drafts must be presented with clean, readable fonts and a capped line width (65–75ch) to optimize reading comfort.
3. **Frictionless Sign-off:** The review, edit, and approve actions should follow a logical top-to-bottom sequence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA compliant contrast ratios for status labels and badges.
- Responsive layout supporting standard desktop and laptop screen sizes.
